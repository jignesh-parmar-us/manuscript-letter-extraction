3 · Training data and the letter reader

The letter reader is a small neural network trained on your reviewed letters. It is written in NumPy (PyTorch does not install on this computer), and it trains in about a minute.

What the training data is

  • Every letter in a labelled group of the books you choose (books of the same writing). By default only groups you reviewed or labelled; also groups not reviewed adds labels given by Tesseract or other books.
  • Read Book letters you corrected in the Text view are in groups too, so they count.
  • Word labels (several aksharas in one box) are left out unless also word labels is ticked.
  • A label needs at least N letters (you choose; 5 by default, down to 1). Fewer: left out and listed, and the reader cannot read that letter at all.
  • Nothing is copied: the reader reads the letters' masks from the book folder and their labels from the database.

Export dataset (Training Data → Export) writes the same data for other tools: every letter of each label in dataset/<category>/<letter>/ (as cut, black on white, or 64 × 64), each line image with its Gujarati text from the labels in lines/ ([?] for an unlabelled letter), letters.csv, samples.csv (traceable to page, line and box) and an overview page.

The reader's features

Finer than the grouping fingerprints, and they keep the letter's size, because the reader must tell a dot from a letter and one letter from two. From the mask:

  1. crop to the ink, pad to a square (aspect ratio kept), resize to 40 × 40, Gaussian blur σ = 0.8;
  2. pixels: the image at 20 × 20 = 400 values (0–1);
  3. HOG of the 40 px image: 6 × 6 cells, 9 direction bins, 2 × 2 block normalisation: 900 values;
  4. size: width / height, width / line spacing, height / line spacing: 3 values.

1,303 numbers per letter, standardised (mean 0, spread 1, from the training letters) before the network.

Varied copies (augmentation)

Each training letter gets copies that vary as print and hand vary, made from its mask: rotation of up to ±4°, slant (shear) of up to ±0.12, strokes 1 px thicker or thinner (2 × 2 dilation or erosion, each a third of the time), and in 30 % of the copies 8 % of the columns trimmed off one side (a cut slightly off). Rare labels get more copies: max(3, min(30, ⌈20 / letters⌉)), so a label with 1 letter gets 20 copies and one with 500 gets 3. Features and copies are computed once per training and reused by its six trainings.

The networks and how they learn

Linear readerNetwork
Shapefeatures → softmax over the labelsfeatures → 384 ReLU units (dropout 0.2) → softmax
Initial weightssmall random (σ 0.01)He initialisation
Losscross-entropy (the negative log probability of the right label)
OptimiserAdam, learning rate 0.002, mini-batches of 128, L2 weight decay 0.0001
Stoppingup to 60 epochs; stops when the loss on the validation pages has not improved for 6 epochs; the weights of the best epoch are kept

Both are trained; the one with the higher accuracy on the validation pages is kept (on the printed book the linear reader won: 96.1 % against 95.3 % on the test pages).

Test and report

  • The kept reader reads the test pages, which it never learned from: overall accuracy, accuracy for labels with 20 or more letters, accuracy per label ("not measured" when a label has no test letter) and the most confused pairs.
  • A label with no letter outside the kept-aside pages is trained on all its letters (so it can still be read), and shows as "not measured".
  • Limits: the test letters are letters of reviewed groups, mostly cleanly cut, so the accuracy describes clean letters, not badly cut ones.

Check these

The pages are split into 4 folds; for each fold a reader of the same kind is trained (12 epochs) on the other three and reads that fold. A letter is listed when that reader reads it as another label with at least 90 % probability. Letters of labelled but unreviewed groups are read by the final reader. In Review groups the list shows only letters still where they were (same label, not deleted); It is right hides one (remembered in the browser).

The final reader

  • Trained on all pages (12 epochs), saved as readers/<folder>/reader.npz (weights, means and spreads) and reader.json (labels, size), recorded in the reader table and made the active reader of the books it learned from.
  • It then reads every Training Data letter of the book: one reading per letter (the most probable label, confidence = probability × 100), stored as the book's reader run. It becomes the book's main source of suggestions; it never splits groups by itself.
  • Each training makes a new reader; older ones are kept; Use for this book switches.

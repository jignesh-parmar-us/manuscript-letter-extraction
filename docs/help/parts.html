The parts explained

Training Data, groups, labels, the letter checker, the language model, the Dictionary, Tesseract, whole lines and line truth: what each one is, what it learns from, where you find it, and whether it helps.

Two kinds of knowledge

Everything that reads your book uses one of two kinds of knowledge:

What letters look like

Tesseract, your groups, your letter checker and our reader look at the image and say which letter it is.

Which letters go together

The language model never looks at the image. It knows which letter sequences are common in your texts, and picks between readings that the others gave.

Where they learn from

Training Data (your labelled letters), your proofread lines and your word lists (Dictionary).

So a reader and the language model are different things: a reader reads shapes, the language model knows words. Some screens use one, some the other, some both.

The line search (no Tesseract; it tries every place a letter could start and end along the line and keeps the best-reading path) gets 10.2% wrong alone: too many to replace Tesseract, but where it disagrees with Tesseract it points at 88% of the remaining mistakes while marking a fifth of the aksharas. Its own text is Whole lines (our reader); its marks show in both Whole lines tabs. It is a different reader from the Letter checker (Training Data), which reads letters already cut and is used by Check these and the hidden Cut letters view.

In short: Tesseract reads, the language model corrects. Groups and labels are how you teach the app your book; the letter checker is useful for finding wrongly grouped letters (Check these), but not for reading the text. That is why Cut letters is hidden in Text since October 2026 (its data is kept; Pages & capture → Settings → Show Cut letters brings it back for experiments).

Short definitions

Akshara
One written unit: a consonant or conjunct with its vowel signs and marks (ક્યા, ર્ત્તા, નં).
Cut letters
The page cut into one image per akshara, so each can be grouped, labelled and read alone. Preparing a book for reading still cuts its letters (our reader needs their ink).
Whole lines
The page cut only into lines; each line image goes to Tesseract as it is.
Uncertain
A letter a reader is not sure about, highlighted in the text and written as [?] in downloads if you want.
Proofread line
A line of the Cut letters text you marked ✓ as right; the language model learns from it.

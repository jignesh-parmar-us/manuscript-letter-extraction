The steps in order

Every book has two parts. In Training Data you teach the app the letters of the book from a few sample pages. In Read Book the whole book is turned into text. Do them in this order. Each box links to its details in Adding a new book.

Part 1 · Training Data

A folder of 20 to 25 sample pages. Done once per book (and topped up later).

1

Create the book

Books → New book: a name, the folder with the sample page images, and Printed or Handwritten.

Before you start →

↓
2

Capture the letters

Training Data → Pages & capture. Printed books: first choose Cutting → By Tesseract's reading. Then Capture letters and wait (about 5 minutes for 23 pages). The pages are cut into lines and letters, and similar letters are put into groups.

↓
3

Give the groups labels

Training Data → Review groups

↓

Printed or handwritten?

printed

Tesseract has already labelled the groups it is sure about. Then: Suggest from labelled books (if you have other printed books) → Split mixed groups → Accept N with ≥ 90 %.

Details →

handwritten

With another labelled handwritten book: Suggest from labelled books → Split mixed groups → Accept. Otherwise label the 30 to 50 biggest groups by hand.

Details →

↓
4

Review the groups

Biggest groups first: look at the letters, move the wrong ones out, fix the label, tick Reviewed. You do not need to finish every group: the big ones matter most.

Details →

↓
5

Train the letter reader

Training Data → Readers → Train (about a minute). It learns your reviewed letters and reports how many it reads right on pages it did not learn from.

Details →

↓
6

Fix what the reader finds

Review groups → Check these: letters the reader is sure are in the wrong group. Move to …, or It is right.

↺ Go round again whenever you like: review more groups (4), fix the letters in Check these (6), then Train again (5). Each training makes a new reader; the older ones are kept.
↓

Part 2 · Read Book

The whole book, as a PDF or a folder of page images.

7

Upload and cut the whole book

Read Book → Upload & capture. The book's PDF (Use this PDF) or a folder of page images, then Cut the pages. It runs in the background; stopping keeps the pages already cut.

Details →

↓
8

Convert the pages into text

Read Book → Text → Convert all pages. Each page is shown beside its text, with uncertain letters highlighted. Tesseract + your groups marks far more of the real mistakes than Tesseract only.

↓
9

Proofread

Click a wrong letter (or Next uncertain ▶), type the right one, Correct. Two letters…, Join with the next and Not a letter fix bad boxes. Click ✓ at the end of each line that is right.

↓
10

Download the text

The page or the whole book as .txt, Word, PDF or a picture, with uncertain letters as [?], [?ક] or the best guess.

↺ Back to Part 1: every letter you correct in step 9 joins its group in Training Data, so the next Train (5) learns from it. A page that reads very badly can be moved to Training Data (Read Book → Book pages → Use for training) and labelled there.
What the reader changes today. The letter reader (step 5) gives the groups their suggestions and finds the letters in Check these. The text of Read Book (step 8) is read by Tesseract and checked against your groups, not by the reader yet. So reviewing groups and fixing Check these improves the text; using the reader for the text is planned next.

Which step do I need?

I want to…Go to
start a new bookSteps 1 to 4, then 5
get the text of a book whose Training Data is doneSteps 7 to 10
make the reader betterReview more groups (4), fix Check these (6), Train again (5)
make the text betterReview groups (4), then Convert again (8); proofread (9)
add another book of the same print or handKeep the labelled book: in the new one, step 3 uses Suggest from labelled books to label it from the old one
Everything can be undone. Undo (or Ctrl/Cmd+Z) takes back any change to groups and letters, including corrections in the text.
